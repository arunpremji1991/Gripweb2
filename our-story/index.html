Our vision

More than great food.

GRIP was founded on the belief that hospitality is about more than serving great food — it is about creating a complete experience that reflects quality, innovation, and authentic Omani identity through a contemporary approach.

Inspired by global best practices in the Smash Burger concept, GRIP has been thoughtfully developed through an Omani vision that emphasizes product quality, attention to detail, and an exceptional customer experience.

The concept is also supported by the founders' practical experience in developing and operating hospitality brands in Dhofar, including Lazurd and LIT LIN, providing a strong foundation for building a distinctive brand with a clear vision for the future.

Our journey

Growing with purpose.

GRIP's journey begins in Dhofar with a long-term vision that prioritizes operational excellence, customer trust, and a consistently high-quality experience before pursuing expansion.

The growth strategy follows a carefully planned path — starting within Dhofar, expanding across the Sultanate of Oman, and ultimately establishing a proudly Omani brand capable of regional and international growth through a sustainable franchise model.

The ambition is not simply to open more locations, but to build a lasting brand that delivers a consistent experience, maintains exceptional quality, and proudly represents Oman wherever it grows.
