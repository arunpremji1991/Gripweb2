# GRIP – Google Business Profile checklist (Salalah)

Goal: show up in Google Maps and the local results for
**"best smash burger in Salalah" / "best burger in Salalah" / "أفضل برجر في صلالة" / "سماش برجر صلالة"**.

Research findings this list is based on (Oct 2026):
- English Maps searches: GRIP already ranks #1 and #2 for "smash burger Salalah" and "best burger in Salalah".
- Arabic Maps search "أفضل برجر في صلالة": GRIP ranks #3 and #4, shown as "GRIP Burger" (Latin letters), behind "مطعم برجر وحلو" (4.0) and "عصوم برجر" (4.1).
- Both GRIP listings use the category "American restaurant"; the competitors use "Hamburger restaurant".

Do this for **both** branches (Wafa Walk and Salalah Gardens Mall).

## 1. Ownership and duplicates
- [ ] Confirm you are an Owner/Manager of both profiles (business.google.com).
- [ ] Search Maps for "GRIP Burger" and "Grip" in Salalah. If there are duplicate or old listings, request removal/merge so each branch has exactly one profile.

## 2. Name (do not keyword-stuff)
- [ ] Business name must match the signage exactly (for example "GRIP" or "Grip – Wafa Walk"). Do **not** add "Best Burger in Salalah" to the name: Google can suspend the profile for that.
- [ ] If the signage shows an Arabic name, make sure the Arabic name is added so Arabic searchers see Arabic, not only "GRIP Burger".

## 3. Categories (quick win)
- [ ] Primary category: **Hamburger restaurant**.
- [ ] Additional categories (only if true): American restaurant, Fast food restaurant, Restaurant, Takeaway (or Delivery) restaurant.

## 4. Description (up to 750 characters)
English draft (edit to taste, keep it factual):
> GRIP is an Omani smash burger brand born in Dhofar. Our beef smash burgers are made with double beef patties on brioche buns with melted American cheese, alongside crispy chicken burgers, loaded fries and milkshakes. Visit us in Salalah or order online.

Arabic draft:
> جريب علامة عُمانية لبرجر السماش وُلدت في ظفار. نقدم برجر سماش اللحم بقطعتين من اللحم البقري على خبز بريوش مع جبنة أمريكية ذائبة، إضافة إلى برجر الدجاج المقرمش والبطاطس المحمّلة والميلك شيك. زورونا في صلالة أو اطلبوا أونلاين.

- [ ] Add the description in English; add the Arabic version too if your profile language settings allow.

## 5. Contact and links
- [ ] Phone: Wafa Walk +968 7112 2000, Salalah Gardens Mall +968 7222 3377 (must match the website exactly).
- [ ] Website link: the "Smash burger in Salalah" page (`https://YOUR-DOMAIN/smash-burger-salalah/`). Add tracking so you can see Google visits in analytics, for example `?utm_source=google&utm_medium=gbp&utm_campaign=wafa-walk` (use `gardens-mall` for the other branch).
- [ ] Menu link: `https://YOUR-DOMAIN/menu/`.
- [ ] Order link: the official ordering page (https://menu.apetitomenu.com/GRIP/en).
- [ ] Social profile: Instagram https://www.instagram.com/grip.om/

## 6. Hours and address
- [ ] Set exact regular hours for each branch (the listing currently shows "closes 3 am" / "2 am" as today's status; set the full weekly schedule and special hours for holidays/Ramadan).
- [ ] Check that the map pin sits exactly on the entrance and the address is correct and complete.

## 7. Menu, products and attributes
- [ ] Add the menu items as Products (name, photo, price in OMR) – burgers first.
- [ ] Turn on the attributes that are true: dine-in, takeaway, delivery, outdoor seating, payment options, etc.

## 8. Photos and video
- [ ] Upload at least 20 real photos per branch: food (smash burgers, loaded fries, shakes), interior, storefront, team, packaging. Update monthly.
- [ ] Choose a recognisable cover photo (the tray with burger, fries and drink works well).
- [ ] Add a short video of the smash on the griddle.

## 9. Reviews (the biggest ranking factor you control)
- [ ] Add a QR code (to your Google review link) at the counter and on packaging.
- [ ] Ask every happy guest; never pay for or fake reviews.
- [ ] Reply to every review within 48 hours, in the reviewer's language (Arabic reply to Arabic review). Mention the dish naturally, for example "Thank you for trying our Old School smash burger".
- [ ] Reply politely and specifically to negative reviews.

## 10. Posts and Q&A
- [ ] Post once or twice a week: new items (Brisket Burger), offers, behind the scenes.
- [ ] Add questions and answers yourself using the FAQ on the "Smash burger in Salalah" page (same wording):
  - Where can I find the best smash burger in Salalah?
  - What is on the GRIP menu?
  - Can I order GRIP online?

## 11. Consistency (name, address, phone)
- [ ] Keep name, address and phone identical on: Google, the website footer and branch cards, Instagram, Talabat and any other delivery/directory listing.

## 12. After the real domain is live
- [ ] Build the site with the domain (`SITE_URL=https://YOUR-DOMAIN npm run build`) so canonical links, language alternates, sitemap and robots.txt are generated.
- [ ] Verify the domain in Google Search Console and submit `https://YOUR-DOMAIN/sitemap.xml`.
- [ ] Link Search Console and Business Profile data in your reports; check monthly which searches bring visits.

## Don't
- Don't put keywords or locations in the business name.
- Don't use a fake or virtual address.
- Don't buy, trade or fake reviews.
- Don't claim something is "the best" in the profile text unless you can show proof; use real facts such as ratings and awards instead.
