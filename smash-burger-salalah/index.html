What is a smash burger?

Pressed hot. Crisp at the edges.

A smash burger starts with a ball of ground beef pressed flat on a very hot griddle. The thin patty sears fast and builds a crisp, caramelised crust while the inside stays juicy.

At GRIP the beef burgers use a double beef patty on a brioche bun, topped with melted American cheese and sauces such as ketchup and mustard, bacon jam or truffle sauce.
